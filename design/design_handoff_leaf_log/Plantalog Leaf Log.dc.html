Turn 3 · 2a folded into the View card

With Leaf Log on, the View card loses its top right corner and runs off the edge. Tap Leaf Log or swipe left: the card slides over and the log fills the right 75%, leaving the card's right quarter showing. Tap that strip or swipe right to go back. Built from the current View and Edit code on main.
